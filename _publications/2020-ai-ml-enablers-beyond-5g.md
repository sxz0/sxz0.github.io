This white paper on AI/ML as enablers of 5G and B5G networks is based on contributions from 5G PPP projects that research, implement and validate 5G and B5G network systems. The white paper introduces the main relevant mechanisms in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning currently investigated and exploited for 5G and beyond 5G networks, elaborates on use cases and optimisation problems tackled with AI/ML (network planning, network diagnostics/insights, and network optimisation and control), discusses the application of AI/ML in the 5G network architecture, and identifies challenges in view of trust in AI/ML-based networks and potential solutions such as the zero-trust management approach.
